How it works

From twenty minutes of downloads to one ranked board

01

Pull the whole slate

Every Baseball Savant leaderboard, last-5 and last-10 splits by pitcher hand, confirmed lineups, bullpen availability and park factors — collected automatically, five times a day.

02

Match bat against arsenal

A pitcher throws a specific mix. A hitter has a record against each pitch type. Weighting one by the other shows how he fares against this starter, not against pitching in general.

03

Rank, then explain

Hits, total bases, home runs, RBIs, runs, strikeouts and game totals — scored on the numbers, each with a one-line reason you can check.
